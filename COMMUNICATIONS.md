## 1. Official Parish Email (Full Announcement)

**Subject:** Join us: Pilgrimage to Our Lady of Mount Grace, Osmotherley – Saturday 31 October

**Preheader:** *A special morning of prayer and entrustment to Our Lady as we conclude the month of the Holy Rosary.*

Dear Parishioners, Friends, and Families,

The **Sacred Heart Youth Group** warmly invites you, your family, and our wider parish community to join us on a pilgrimage to the historic shrine of **Our Lady of Mount Grace, Osmotherley**, on **Saturday 31 October 2026**.

October is the month specially dedicated to Our Lady, and this pilgrimage offers us a blessed opportunity to step away together in faith. We will place before Our Lady our loved ones, personal intentions, hopes, plans, and the needs of our families and community, seeking her maternal intercession as we draw closer to her Son, Jesus Christ.

### Key Event Details:
* 📅 **Date:** Saturday, 31 October 2026
* ⏰ **Time:** 11:00 am – 12:30 pm *(Please arrive by 10:45 am for the gathering)*
* 📍 **Venue:** Our Lady of Mount Grace Catholic Church, Monastery of Our Lady of Mt Grace, 18 North End, Osmotherley, Northallerton, DL6 3BB
* 🗺️ **Google Maps Directions:** [Click here for directions](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BJSogD4n3oQs6GMQ7)
* 🌐 **Online Registration & Prayer Intentions:** [Visit our Pilgrimage Portal](index.html)

### Schedule of the Morning:
* **10:45 am** – Arrival, welcome, and gathering at the foot of the woodland path
* **11:00 am** – The Angelus and Opening Marian Hymn
* **11:15 am** – Meditated Holy Rosary (offering our intentions before Our Lady)
* **11:45 am** – Litany of Loreto & Entrustment of Families and Youth
* **12:00 pm** – Silent contemplation, lighting of votive candles, and blessing
* **12:30 pm** – Closing prayer, followed by shared fellowship & walks on the Cleveland Moors

### Important Pilgrim Advice:
* **Everyone is welcome:** All ages—children, youth, adults, and seniors.
* **Footwear & Attire:** The final ascent to the historic Lady Chapel is along a gentle stone woodland track. Please wear comfortable, sturdy walking shoes and warm autumn layers.
* **Carpooling:** If you need a lift or can offer spare seats in your car, please let us know when registering.
* **Prayer Intentions:** Even if you cannot attend in person, you can submit your prayer intentions via our website or text, and they will be prayerfully placed before the shrine.

### RSVP & Contact:
To help us coordinate numbers and transport, please register online or contact **Ozurumba Lucia** directly:
* **Phone / WhatsApp:** 07423 239193
* Alternatively, speak directly with **Fr Mike** after Sunday Mass.

*A copy of the pilgrimage flyer is attached. Please feel free to forward this invitation to family and friends, or print a copy for your noticeboard.*

We look forward to journeying together in prayer under Our Lady’s mantle.

With every blessing,

**The Sacred Heart Youth Group**  
*Sacred Heart Catholic Church*
